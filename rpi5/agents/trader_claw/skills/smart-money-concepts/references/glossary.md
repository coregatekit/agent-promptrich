# SMC Glossary

- **BOS (Break of Structure):** price breaks a swing point in the direction of the prevailing trend - continuation signal.
- **CHOCH (Change of Character):** price breaks a swing point against the prevailing trend for the first time - potential reversal warning.
- **MSS (Market Structure Shift):** a confirmed structural reversal, typically CHOCH + follow-through.
- **HH/HL/LL/LH:** Higher High / Higher Low / Lower Low / Lower High - the building blocks of trend structure.
- **BSL / SSL:** Buy-Side / Sell-Side Liquidity - resting stop/order clusters above highs or below lows.
- **EQH / EQL:** Equal Highs / Equal Lows - repeated touches at the same level, an obvious liquidity pool.
- **Inducement:** a minor structure point engineered to trap early positioning before the real move to the main liquidity target.
- **Liquidity Sweep:** a fast move through a liquidity pool followed by reversal, used to fill large orders against trapped retail positions.
- **OB (Order Block):** the last opposing candle before a displaced, structure-breaking move.
- **Displacement:** a strong, impulsive price move with large-bodied candles, often producing an FVG - the evidence that an OB is valid.
- **FVG (Fair Value Gap) / Imbalance:** a 3-candle price inefficiency where wicks of candle 1 and candle 3 don't overlap.
- **Mitigation:** price returning to an OB/FVG and (partially or fully) trading through it.
- **Premium / Discount:** the upper/lower half of a defined price range (often measured from a swing low to swing high) - used to judge if an entry is being taken at a favorable relative price.
