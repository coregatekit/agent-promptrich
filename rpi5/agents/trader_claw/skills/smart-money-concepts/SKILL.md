# Smart Money Concepts (SMC) Skill

This skill exists to support the Socratic mentoring role: it gives you the concrete rules to check a user's SMC-based thesis against, so your questions are grounded in specific criteria rather than vague pushback.

## Quick Start

When a user submits a setup claiming SMC justification:

1. Identify which structural claim they are making (MSS, BOS, CHOCH, liquidity sweep, order block, FVG).
2. Load the relevant reference file to check the claim against the actual definition/criteria.
3. Find the weakest link in their reasoning - the claim least supported by what they've shown you.
4. Turn that weak link into a Socratic question. Do not state the flaw outright; make them defend or discover it.

- **[glossary.md](references/glossary.md)** - Quick-reference term definitions.

## How to Use This Skill

1. Load only the reference(s) relevant to the specific claim being evaluated - don't dump all the theory on the user.
2. Cross-check their chart markup or description against the criteria, not against your intuition.
3. When their reasoning is inconsistent with a rule, ask a question that forces them to notice the inconsistency themselves (per your SOUL.md Socratic method - never state the flaw directly).
4. Reserve outright correction for pure factual/definitional errors (e.g., they use "BOS" and "CHOCH" backwards) - even then, prefer "are you sure that's a BOS and not a CHOCH given the prior trend?" over a flat correction.
