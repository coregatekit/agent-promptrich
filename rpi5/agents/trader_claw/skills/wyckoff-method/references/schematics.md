# Wyckoff Schematics: Accumulation and Distribution (Phase A-E)

## Accumulation (bottoming structure, smart money buying)

- **Phase A - Stopping the prior downtrend:**
  - PS (Preliminary Support): first sign of buying interest after a decline, doesn't stop the trend but hints at it.
  - SC (Selling Climax): sharp panic sell-off, often on very high volume, wide spread down, often a long wick as buyers absorb aggressively.
  - AR (Automatic Rally): sharp rebound off the SC as selling pressure exhausts.
  - ST (Secondary Test): price revisits SC area, usually on lower volume, to test supply.

- **Phase B - Building the cause:**
  - Range-bound trading between the SC/ST low and AR high. Smart money accumulates.
  - Multiple STs may occur. This phase can be long and choppy - it's where users most often mistake noise for a signal.

- **Phase C - The test:**
  - **Spring:** price dips below the established range low (below SC/ST), shaking out weak longs and triggering stops, then reclaims the range quickly. Ideally on lower volume than the SC, with quick recovery.
  - A spring is the "final shakeout" - a defining, high-value Wyckoff signal *if* volume and speed of recovery confirm it.
  - Not every dip below range is a spring - a low-volume slow grind below range that stays there is a breakdown, not a spring.

- **Phase D - Markup begins within the range:**
  - SOS (Sign of Strength): a rally through range resistance on increased volume/spread, confirming demand is in control.
  - LPS (Last Point of Support): a higher low / pullback after SOS that holds well above the spring low, on light volume - the last cheap entry before markup.

- **Phase E - Markup / trend departure:**
  - Price leaves the range decisively, trending up.

## Distribution (topping structure, smart money selling) - mirror image

- **Phase A:** PSY (Preliminary Supply), BC (Buying Climax - high volume blow-off top), AR (Automatic Reaction down), ST (Secondary Test near BC high).
- **Phase B:** range-bound distribution, building cause for markdown.
- **Phase C:** **UTAD (Upthrust After Distribution)** - price spikes above range high (mirror of spring), traps late longs, then fails back into range. Ideally on climactic volume with a fast rejection.
- **Phase D:** SOW (Sign of Weakness) - break of range support on increased volume/spread; LPSY (Last Point of Supply) - lower high pullback on light volume.
- **Phase E:** markdown / trend departure down.

## Key Discipline

A Wyckoff read is only as strong as the phase sequence supporting it. Demand the user show Phase A and B context before accepting a Phase C event (Spring/UTAD) as valid - an isolated "spring-looking" candle with no preceding range/climax is not Wyckoff, it's pattern-matching.
