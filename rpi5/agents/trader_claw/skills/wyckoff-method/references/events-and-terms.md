# Wyckoff Events and Terms

## Accumulation Events

- **PS (Preliminary Support):** early buying that slows a decline without stopping it.
- **SC (Selling Climax):** panic, high-volume capitulation low.
- **AR (Automatic Rally):** sharp bounce off SC as selling dries up; its high often defines the top of the trading range.
- **ST (Secondary Test):** retest of SC area, ideally on lower volume with a narrower spread than SC - shows diminishing supply.
- **Spring:** a decisive break below the range low that quickly reverses back inside the range, shaking out remaining weak hands.
- **Test:** a lower-volume retest of the spring low to confirm supply is gone.
- **SOS (Sign of Strength):** strong up-move through range resistance on rising volume - demand has taken control.
- **LPS (Last Point of Support):** a shallow, low-volume pullback after SOS - the last low-risk long entry before markup.

## Distribution Events (mirror)

- **PSY (Preliminary Supply):** first sign of selling interest at highs.
- **BC (Buying Climax):** panic/euphoric high-volume top.
- **AR (Automatic Reaction):** sharp drop off BC.
- **ST:** retest of BC high, ideally on lower volume.
- **UTAD (Upthrust After Distribution):** decisive break above range high that fails and reverses back inside - traps late longs.
- **SOW (Sign of Weakness):** strong down-move through range support on rising volume.
- **LPSY (Last Point of Supply):** shallow, low-volume pullback up after SOW - the last low-risk short entry before markdown.

## Common Misuse to Challenge

- Calling any dip a "spring" regardless of prior range context or volume signature.
- Calling a SOS/SOW valid without a volume increase - a break on light volume is more likely a fakeout.
- Treating ST as a second chance to enter without confirming supply/demand is actually diminishing (compare spread and volume to the SC/BC, not just "it happened again").
