# Wyckoff Volume Analysis: Effort vs. Result

## The Core Principle

Volume (effort) and price spread/movement (result) should agree. When they disagree, it signals something about who's in control.

- **Effort = Result (agreement):** high volume + wide spread in the direction of the move → the move is genuine, trend likely continues.
- **Effort > Result (disagreement):** high volume but little price progress → absorption. Someone large is absorbing supply/demand against the apparent direction - often precedes a reversal.
- **Effort < Result:** large price move on surprisingly low volume → the move may be unsustainable/thin, prone to snapping back.

## Expected Volume Signatures at Key Events

| Event | Expected Volume | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SC / BC | Very high (climactic) | Panic/euphoria, capitulation |
| AR | Moderate-high | Sharp reaction as pressure releases |
| ST | Lower than SC/BC | Diminishing supply/demand confirms the climax held |
| Spring / UTAD | Often lower than SC/BC, with fast reversal | A genuine shakeout doesn't need heavy volume to fail - it needs speed of recovery |
| SOS / SOW | Rising, above recent average | Real participation confirming new direction |
| LPS / LPSY | Low | Lack of supply/demand on the pullback is bullish (LPS) or bearish (LPSY) confirmation |

## Red Flags to Push the User On

- A "spring" on heavy volume that recovers slowly - this looks more like a failed breakdown attempt than an engineered shakeout; the range may actually be breaking.
- An "SOS" on volume below average - ask them to justify why they trust the breakout without participation.
- Ignoring volume entirely and reasoning off price shape alone - Wyckoff without volume is just pattern drawing.

## Questions to Ask

- "What did volume do at the moment you're calling [event]? Does it match what that event requires?"
- "Compare the volume at this ST to the SC/BC - is supply/demand actually drying up, or is it about the same?"
- "If this were the effort you're describing, where's the result? And if the result doesn't match, what does that tell you about who's really in control?"
